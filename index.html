Two modes

Online, every match is drawn at random from the modes and maps you pick, at least two of each. Against bots, pick as few as one.

BOMBFirst to 5 rounds

TDMFirst to 60 kills

BOMB

Plant it or defuse it. One life a round: nobody respawns until the next one. First to 5 rounds wins, and sides swap after round 4.

TEAM DEATHMATCH

Nonstop fighting. Go down and you're back in 3 seconds, somewhere no enemy can see you. First to 60 kills wins, or the most kills when the 7 minutes run out.

How a bomb round plays

Attackers plant one bomb at site A or site B. Defenders stop the plant or defuse it. Nobody respawns until the next round.

  1. 6 sFreezeLook around, change your loadout, call the plan.
  2. 100 sPushAttackers carry the bomb toward a site. Defenders split up and hold.
  3. 4 sPlantHold inside the site's outline, crouched. Anyone close can stop you.
  4. 40 sFuseThe beeping speeds up. Defenders have to take the site back.
  5. 7 sDefuseHold next to the bomb before the fuse runs out.

First team to 5 rounds wins. Sides swap after round 4.

Turn into a spider bot any time

Get out of a lost fight, beat the other team to a site or slip through a crawl vent. A spider can't shoot, but it can be shot.

  • On foot4.8 m/s
  • Knife out5.8 m/s
  • Spider bot7.5 m/s
  • 0.2 s to turn into a spider, 0.35 s to turn back.
  • Crawl vents link the lanes on both maps.
