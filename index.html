Gameplay

A real match on PRIMETIME PLAZA, filmed as it played. Turn the sound on.

Hold the most zones

The arena has five zones, A to E. Stand in one to pull its bar to your side. Each zone your team holds scores a point every 2.5 seconds, whether anyone stays in it or not.

  1. TakeStand inside. Alone you fill it in 5 seconds, five of you in 1.
  2. HoldIt stays yours until the other team drains it back to neutral, and it scores the whole time.
  3. ContestWith both teams inside, the bar stops. Clear them out to get it moving again.
  4. SwapAt 150 points or five minutes, sides swap and every zone goes neutral.
  5. WinFirst team to 300 wins. Go down and you're back in 5 seconds.

Roll and climb

Tap ROLL and you dodge three metres the way you're moving, then come up with the gun ready. Slip out of a duel, cross an open lane or dive into cover. Then take the high ground: pull yourself up onto ledges, climb ladders and ride ziplines to the rooftops.

  • On foot5.0 m/s
  • Knife out6.0 m/s
  • Roll7.5 m/s
  • Ready again a moment after you land, sooner with a light weapon in hand.
  • Ammo never runs out either. Reload and get back in.

Four skill devices

Every device is free: pick one and change it any time in the loadout. Two skills and a passive each. Play as a woman or a man with the face, hair and outfit you pick: what you can do comes from your device, never from your look.

Skill device · M6 AURA

MEDIC

MEDI-BOTSends out a hovering drone that follows you and heals teammates close by.

ADRENALINERun and fire 25% faster for 4 seconds.

REGENHealth comes back 3 seconds after the fighting stops.

Skill device · G3 SHADE

GHOST

CLOAKFades out for 15 seconds. Still or crouched, almost nothing shows; moving or firing gives a shimmer away.

BLINKTeleports up to 8 m the way you look.

SILENT STEPSYour footsteps make no sound.

Skill device · R1 ARGUS

RECON

SCANSweeps sonar 30 m ahead, through walls. Up to five enemies in it show up for your whole team.

FRAGA grenade you throw along a line you can see: hold to aim, let go to throw. It bounces and goes off 2.2 seconds later.

TRACKEREvery enemy you hit shows to your team for 2 seconds.

Skill device · T2 VOLT

TECH

BARRIERRaises an energy wall that stops bullets but lets people walk through.

EMPA pulse round you: enemies in it can't use skills for 4 seconds.

ARMOR PLATING120 health instead of 100.

Capture on PRIMETIME PLAZA

Online, every match plays on PRIMETIME PLAZA. In a custom game you set the rules, with friends or against bots.

Capture

Five zones. First to 300 points, and the sides swap at halftime.

Each half of the arena is the other half turned around, so neither side starts ahead.
