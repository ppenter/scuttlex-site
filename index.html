Hold the most zones

Every map has five zones, A to E. Stand in one to pull its bar to your side. Each zone your team holds scores a point every 2.5 seconds, whether anyone stays in it or not.

  1. TakeStand inside. Alone you fill it in 5 seconds, all five of you in 1.
  2. HoldIt stays yours until the other team drains it back to neutral, and it scores the whole time.
  3. ContestWith both teams inside, the bar stops. Clear them out to get it moving again.
  4. SwapAt 150 points or five minutes, sides swap and every zone goes neutral.
  5. WinFirst team to 300 wins. Go down and you're back in 5 seconds.

Spider dash

Tap DASH and your hero folds into its spider bot, scuttles five metres and stands back up with the gun ready, all in a quarter of a second. Slip out of a duel, cross an open lane or reach a zone first.

  • On foot5.5 m/s
  • Knife out6.6 m/s
  • Spider dash20 m/s
  • Two charges. Each one comes back 5 seconds after you use it.
  • Tap again while you're still a spider and it carries straight on.

Two modes, two maps

Online, every match is drawn from the modes and maps you pick. In a custom game you set the mode, the map and the rules, with friends or against bots.

Capture

Five zones. First to 300 points, and the sides swap at halftime.

Team Deathmatch

Nonstop fighting, back in 5 seconds where no enemy can see you. First to 60 kills, or the most when the 7 minutes run out.

Each half of a map is the other half turned around, so neither side starts ahead.

Real guns, arcade rules
