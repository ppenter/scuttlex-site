SCUTTLE X · Free to play

Get the game

SCUTTLE X on iPhone, iPad, Android, Mac or Windows, free. Phones and tablets play together, computers with computers.

Your device

iPhone & iPad

iOS / iPadOS 15 or later · the closed beta on TestFlight

  1. Install TestFlight from the App Store: Apple's free app for trying games before they come out.
  2. Tap Join the beta on TestFlight below, then Accept and Install.
  3. Updates come through TestFlight. Places are limited.
Join the beta on TestFlight

Your device

Android

64-bit phones, Android 10 or later · the APK, straight from us

  1. Tap Download APK, then open the file when it's done.
  2. If your phone asks, allow your browser to install apps.
  3. If Google Play Protect warns about it, tap More details, then Install anyway: the file is ours, signed by SCUTTLE X.
  4. When a new version is out, the game brings you back here. Install it over the old one: your account stays.

Prefer Google Play? Join its beta: join the tester group, then become a tester. Pick one: the two copies can't update each other, and switching means uninstalling, which starts a new guest account.

Your computer

Mac

macOS 13 or later · Apple silicon and Intel

  1. Download the file, open it and drag SCUTTLE X onto Applications.
  2. Open SCUTTLE X from Applications. Mouse and keyboard.
  3. Small updates download by themselves when the game opens. When a new version is out, the game brings you back here: install it over the old one, your account stays.

Your computer

Windows

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit · one file, nothing to install

  1. Download the file and open it. Keep it wherever you like: the desktop works.
  2. If Windows says it protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway: the file is ours, it just isn't signed with a paid certificate yet.
  3. Small updates download by themselves when the game opens. When a new version is out, the game brings you back here: use the new file in place of the old one, your account stays.

Good to know

  • Free to play: earn credits by playing to unlock weapons. On iPhone and iPad, diamonds are bought through the App Store; on Android they aren't sold yet.
  • On a computer you play as a guest: your account is kept on that computer (signing in with Apple or Google is on the phones), and diamonds aren't sold there yet. Computers are matched with computers, so mouse aim never meets thumbs.
  • The beta plays on the real servers with everyone else. When the game opens on the App Store, the beta's accounts and progress start over. Purchases in TestFlight are Apple's test ones: nothing is charged.
  • Found a bug? Tell us in the Facebook Group or at support@scuttlex.com.

What the game collects is in the Privacy Policy.
